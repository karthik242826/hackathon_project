About Us

Trusted by Thousands.
Built on Compassion.

HMS Hospital is a leading multi-specialty healthcare institution committed to delivering exceptional patient care through innovation, expertise, and compassion.

Our state-of-the-art facilities, combined with a team of over 120 specialist doctors, ensure that every patient receives world-class diagnosis and treatment.

  • Advanced diagnostic imaging & lab services
  • 24/7 emergency & trauma care
  • Minimally invasive robotic surgery
  • Comprehensive outpatient & inpatient care
  • Digital health records & prescription management
